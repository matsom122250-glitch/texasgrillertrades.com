About & methodology

Texas Griller Trades is a free, public resource that reports stock trades disclosed by members of Congress and by corporate insiders.

Where the data comes from

  • House of Representatives: Periodic Transaction Reports published by the Office of the Clerk.
  • Senate: Periodic Transaction Reports from the Senate electronic financial disclosure (eFD) system.
  • Corporate insiders: SEC Form 4 filings from EDGAR.

What to keep in mind

  • Under the STOCK Act, members of Congress have up to 45 days to report a trade, so disclosures arrive after the fact.
  • Members report dollar ranges (for example $1,001 – $15,000), not exact amounts.
  • Trades may be made by a spouse, dependent, or managed account.
  • Records are collected automatically and may contain parsing errors; always check the original filing.

Not investment advice

Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. It reports public disclosures for informational and news purposes.
