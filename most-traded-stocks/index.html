How these lists differ

  • Congress & insiders counts trades disclosed in public filings that day. Members of Congress report trades up to 45 days after they happen, and insiders within two business days, so this shows what was reported today, not necessarily traded today.
  • Whole market ranks stocks by the number of shares that changed hands in the latest trading session. Heavily traded stocks are often cheap per share or in the news.

Data comes from public disclosures: the U.S. House Clerk, the Senate eFD system, and SEC EDGAR Form 4 filings. Congressional reports can lag trades by up to 45 days. Amounts are the ranges members report, not exact values.
