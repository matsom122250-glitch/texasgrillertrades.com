Upcoming U.S. Treasury auctions

The Treasury's public auction calendar data feed hasn't been updated recently, so it currently lists no upcoming auctions. Treasury auctions themselves continue on their regular schedule. Until the feed updates, see the TreasuryDirect upcoming auctions page. This page updates automatically as soon as the feed does.

How to read this

  • Bills mature in a year or less, notes in 2 to 10 years, and bonds in 20 or 30 years. TIPS are adjusted for inflation.
  • A reopening sells more of a security that already exists, so it shares the original CUSIP and has a slightly shorter term than its name.
  • Offering sizes are set a few days before each auction; until then they show as "to be announced."
  • Settles is the issue date, when buyers pay and receive the securities.

Source: U.S. Department of the Treasury, Fiscal Data “Upcoming Auctions” dataset. Updated hourly on this site. Not investment advice.

Data comes from public disclosures: the U.S. House Clerk, the Senate eFD system, and SEC EDGAR Form 4 filings. Congressional reports can lag trades by up to 45 days. Amounts are the ranges members report, not exact values.
