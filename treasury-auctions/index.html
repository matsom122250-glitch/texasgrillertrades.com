How to read this

  • Bills mature in a year or less, notes in 2 to 10 years, and bonds in 20 or 30 years. TIPS are adjusted for inflation.
  • A reopening sells more of a security that already exists, so it shares the original CUSIP and has a slightly shorter term than its name.
  • Offering sizes are set a few days before each auction; until then they show as "to be announced."
  • Settles is the issue date, when buyers pay and receive the securities.
